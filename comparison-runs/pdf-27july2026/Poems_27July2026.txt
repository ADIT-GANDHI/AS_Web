Banjaara (/uni092C/uni0902/uni091C/uni093E/uni0930/uni093E, Ba□jārā)
Songs addressed to the wandering 
trader or banjaara are a genre of 
folk songs in Rajasthan and 
Malwa. Often ideas from bhakti 
traditions and Kabir percolate into 
these songs, imploring the 
banjaara to end his deluded 
wanderings and revel in the 
wondrous beauties of his own 
home.
